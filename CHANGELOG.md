# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-21

- Redesigned interface: fluid device drawer you can drag and interrupt, translucent materials, tighter typography.
- Installable as an app: "Install Kaa" in Chrome, "Add to Dock" in Safari.
- Radio device groups open by default so every device name is visible.
- Honours reduced motion, reduced transparency and increased contrast.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-20

First public release.

- Device map with the evidence behind every identification.
- Security checks, health score and alerts, including known exploited flaws (CISA KEV).
- Internet card: live latency, trends, per-device presence over 7 days.
- Optional AI analyst, Internet exposure check, Tado and Netatmo radio devices.
- macOS menu bar icon and background service, English and French.
